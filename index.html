FAQ

Straight answers.

Everything you need to know before applying. Still have questions? Message us on Telegram.

How do I get paid?

All partners are paid on a Net-15 basis — payouts are processed within 15 days of month end. You can choose EUR, USD, GBP or USDT. Payment methods include bank wire, crypto (USDT TRC20/ERC20), and eWallet. Your earnings statement is sent before payment for review.

What GEOs do you cover?

We actively accept traffic from 40+ GEOs. Top markets include DE, CA, AU, NZ, NO, FI, NL, SE, AT, CH, PL and more. Some brands have GEO restrictions due to licensing — your account manager will confirm which offers work for your traffic before you go live. We do not accept traffic from the US, France, Israel, Spain (casino), Denmark or the UK — see the full restricted list on our Brands page.

Do you accept Models Agency traffic?

Yes — Models Agency is one of our core verticals. We have a dedicated programme for models, cam performers and OnlyFans creators. Creator audiences are adult, engaged and used to paying for entertainment online. The programme is private by design — your identity is never disclosed to casino brands; your real identity is used only for payout KYC, by FoxyPartners alone.

How fast is the approval process?

We review every application personally and aim to respond within 24 hours — often the same day. Once approved, your account manager will contact you via email or Telegram to confirm your commission plan, set up tracking links, and get you live within 48 hours total.

What is the minimum payout threshold?

The minimum payout is €100 (or currency equivalent). Any balance below the threshold rolls over to the next payment cycle — it is never lost. There is no maximum payout cap.

Do you accept sub-affiliates?

Yes. Our sub-affiliate programme pays you 5% of the net commissions earned by every publisher you refer to FoxyPartners — for life, with no cap. Sub-affiliate earnings are tracked via your unique referral link in the dashboard and paid on the same Net-15 cycle as your main commissions.

What is the cookie duration?

30 days from first click, last-click attribution model. If a user clicks your link and converts within 30 days, the commission is credited to your account. Players who return directly after the initial referral are tracked as long as they are registered under your affiliate tag.

Can I run multiple brands at the same time?

Absolutely. Once approved, you have access to all active FoxyPartners brands through a single account and dashboard. You can run different commission models on different brands simultaneously — for example CPA on FoxyWin and RevShare on another brand. Your AM will help you optimise allocation by GEO.
