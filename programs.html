FAQ

Common questions.

What is RevShare and how is it calculated?

RevShare (Revenue Share) means you earn a percentage of the Net Gaming Revenue (NGR) generated by players you referred. NGR = gross bets minus winnings minus bonuses minus fees. You earn your percentage on the remaining positive NGR each calendar month.

What is negative carryover?

Negative carryover means if a player wins big in one month and your NGR goes negative, that negative balance carries to the next month before you earn again. Some brands allow us to waive this — ask your AM at the time of sign-up. We will always be transparent about the policy before you commit.

When exactly do I get paid?

Payouts are processed on a Net-15 basis. That means if your earnings for June are confirmed by July 1st, payment is processed by July 15th. You'll receive a statement before the payment date for verification.

Can I switch from CPA to RevShare later?

Yes, in most cases. We assess traffic quality after 30–60 days. If your players are showing strong retention and NGR, we can discuss migrating to RevShare or Hybrid. Contact your AM to discuss the terms.

How do you track my referrals?

Via a unique tracking link assigned to your account. All clicks, registrations, FTDs and NGR events are tracked server-to-server (S2S) for maximum accuracy. You have real-time access to all data in the partner dashboard.

Is there a minimum traffic volume requirement?

No minimum volume to join. However, CPA rates and RevShare tiers are volume-dependent — the more quality FTDs you deliver, the better rate you unlock. New partners start at the base tier and are reviewed monthly.

What GEOs do you accept?

We accept traffic from 40+ GEOs. Top GEOs include DE, CA, AU, NZ, NO, FI, NL, UK, AT and more. Some brands are GEO-restricted due to licencing — your AM will clarify per brand. We do not accept US, FR, or IL traffic for casino brands.

What traffic sources are allowed?

SEO, social media, email marketing, influencer content, banner/native ads, and direct traffic. Incentivised traffic, brand bidding, and unsolicited spam are strictly prohibited and will result in account suspension.
