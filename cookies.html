Cookie Policy

Which cookies this site and our tracking links use, and how to control them.

Effective date: 11 October 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. Similar technologies such as local storage, pixels and tracking parameters work in comparable ways. This policy covers all of them.

Cookies on this website

Strictly necessary: cookies needed for the Site to function, such as remembering your menu and form state and protecting the application form from abuse. These cannot be switched off.

Analytics: we may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how visitors use the Site — pages visited, device type, referring source. Where this requires consent under local law we will ask for it before setting such cookies.

This Site does not use advertising or retargeting cookies.

Affiliate tracking cookies

When a player clicks a FoxyPartners tracking link, a cookie is set on the player's device so that a subsequent registration and deposit on the brand can be attributed to the referring partner. The attribution window is 30 days from first click, on a last-click basis. These cookies contain an affiliate identifier and a click timestamp; they do not contain personal data about the player. The brand's own cookie policy governs cookies set on the brand's site.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the Site working. Blocking tracking cookies on a brand site will prevent attribution of that player to a partner.

More information

For details of how we handle personal data see our Privacy Policy.
